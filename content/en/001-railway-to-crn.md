## Outline

1. **The Railway Problem** — How do you make sure two trains never collide? Relay-based interlocking systems as finite state automata.

2. **Electricity as Logic** — No digital chips. Current flows or it doesn't. Relays compute safety constraints in real time.

3. **From Discrete to Continuous** — The leap from relay logic (discrete states) to analog computing (continuous signals). Steam pressure regulation as analog computation.

4. **GPAC: The Mathematical Framework** — Shannon's General Purpose Analog Computer. What polynomial ODEs can compute.

5. **Chemistry as Computer** — Chemical Reaction Networks implementing arithmetic. Anderson & Joshi's result: subtraction is no slower than addition.

6. **The Common Thread** — From railroad switches to molecular concentrations: letting physics do the math.

7. **Looking Forward** — What does "bounded analog complexity" mean? Can we measure how hard a computation is for a chemical system?
